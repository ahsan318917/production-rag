ABC Bank Fixed Deposit Policy

Overview
ABC Bank offers fixed deposits from 1 month to 5 years.

Eligibility
- Individual and joint accounts
- Valid CNIC
- Minimum age 18

Deposit Amount
Minimum: 50,000 PKR
Maximum: No limit

Profit Rates
1 Month: 10%
3 Months: 11%
6 Months: 12%
1 Year: 13%
3 Years: 13.5%
5 Years: 14%

Premature Encashment
Early withdrawal is allowed with a reduced profit rate.

Nomination
Customers may nominate one beneficiary.
